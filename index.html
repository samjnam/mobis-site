Company overview

Where Movement Begins

Hyundai Mobis is a global automotive parts supplier focused on electrification, autonomous driving and connectivity. It builds three core modules (chassis, cockpit and front-end) and supplies service parts for Hyundai, Genesis and Kia vehicles worldwide.

In North America, MOBIS companies manufacture modules and electrified components for Hyundai and Kia assembly plants in Alabama and Georgia, and support engineering, sales and parts operations across the region.

About MOBIS

Lead the Shift in Mobility, Move the World beyond Possibilities.

The Hyundai Mobis brand vision, guiding a global parts supplier on its path toward becoming a mobility solutions provider.

Corporate · Hyundai Mobis

Hyundai Mobis at a glance

Hyundai Mobis forms the parts and service arm for Hyundai, Genesis and Kia. It manufactures the three core vehicle modules (chassis, cockpit and front-end), produces safety, steering and multimedia systems, and supplies after-sales service parts. It has expanded into smart mobility, urban air mobility and robotics.

The company is evolving into a mobility solutions provider, combining software and hardware and pursuing open innovation through startup investment and partnerships with global technology companies.

Its sustainability vision, "Innovation with Responsibility, Mobility by Clean Technology," includes carbon neutrality by 2045 and renewable energy at all business sites by 2040.

Regional · North America

MOBIS in North America

MOBIS operates in North America through several companies. Each is a separate legal entity with its own operations and recruiting portal. This site brings their information together without implying they are one company.

MOBIS Alabama

Module manufacturing in Montgomery since 2002; largest Tier 1 supplier to Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama and Kia Georgia.

MOBIS Savannah

A plant of MOBIS Alabama, LLC in Ellabell, Georgia; largest Tier 1 supplier to Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America.

MOBIS Georgia

Plant in West Point since 2009; largest Tier 1 supplier to Kia Georgia.

MOBIS North America

Regional operations in Michigan and Ohio, the Technical Center of America in Plymouth, and Electrified Powertrain in Richmond Hill, Georgia.

Supply chain

How modularization helps automakers

The Hyundai Mobis Module Division supplies modules just in sequence (JIS) and takes part in the early stages of new vehicle development, which reduces part counts and weight, simplifies assembly and improves inventory control.

Tier 1 module supplier

Complete modules delivered to Hyundai and Kia plants in Alabama and Georgia.

Electrified components

EV battery modules and power electric systems for North American plants.

Global R&D

Research centers in Korea, North America, Europe, China and India.

Service parts

About 2.4 million items stocked for more than 193 vehicle models.

Code of Conduct

Our standard for decisions

The Hyundai Mobis Code of Conduct guides directors, officers and employees to work fairly and responsibly. It brings together the ethics charter, ethical practices and global compliance guidelines, covering conflicts of interest, insider trading, company assets, contracts, and anti-corruption rules on bribery, gifts and hospitality, and political neutrality.

A separate Code of Conduct for Business Partners sets expectations for suppliers and partners.
